Hi! My name is Samantha, but you can call me Sam.

I'm a pediatric occupational therapist (OT) who is passionate about supporting children in a neurodiversity-affirming and strengths-based way.

In my practice, I focus on building a supportive relationship with your child and use a child-centered approach as a gentle alternative to traditional behavioral methods often used with children. My hope is that your child feels understood, supported, and capable. I incorporate play-based activities tailored to their strengths and interests, creating the "just right challenge" to help them grow at their own pace.

Licenses & Certifications

  • Licensed Occupational Therapist in Quebec, Canada
  • Member of L'Ordre des ergothérapeutes du Québec
  • Certified in CPR and first aid
  • Certified in Non-Violent Crisis Prevention and Intervention (CPI)

Areas of Knowledge and Common Areas I Support

Sensory Processing

  • Frequent movement seeking
  • Mouthing or chewing non-food items
  • Sound sensitivities
  • Identifying body signals (hunger, pain, needing to use the bathroom)

Exploring the root causes of challenging behavior

  • Aggression towards self or others
  • Demand avoidance / PDA
  • Anxiety

Fine Motor Skills & Hand-Eye Coordination

  • Pre-writing and handwriting
  • Grasp patterns (pencil, utensils, etc)
  • Scissor skills
  • Using clothing fasteners (zippers, buttons, shoelaces)

Self-Regulation & Co-Regulation

  • Exploring regulation tools & strategies
  • Regulating energy level
  • Learning to support your child through big emotions

Daily living skills

  • Dressing
  • Hygiene (toileting, toothbrushing, bathing)
  • Daily routines

Understanding autism and ADHD from an affirming perspective

  • Play and learning styles
  • Sensory needs
  • Social preferences
  • Executive functioning

School work / activity adaptation

  • Exploring supports and strategies that make daily activities and learning more accessible
  • Exploring supports for understanding instructions, task initiation, breaking tasks down into smaller steps, etc.

Gross Motor Skills

  • Balance and coordination
  • Body awareness
  • Physical strength
  • Movement planning

Environmental adaptations

  • Changing the physical, sensory, social aspects of an environment to better help your child participate and succeed

Services

  • Evaluation & Report

    During the initial session, I will assess your child in the areas that you have identified as needing support. A formal written report is optional and only provided upon request for an additional fee.

  • Occupational Therapy Sessions

    Occupational therapy sessions are 55 minutes and take place in your home or another pre-determined location. During these sessions, I work directly with your child on their goals, using their strengths and interests to make learning fun and meaningful.

  • Caregiver Support & Coaching

    Available in 30- or 60-minute sessions, these online meetings provide an opportunity for us to discuss your child’s areas of difficulty and explore strategies to support them.
